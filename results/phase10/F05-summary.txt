INTERPRETATION:
F05 confirms strict layer isolation inside the UE:
- The UE is registered and both PDU sessions remain established.
- Blackholing one destination removes reachability to that destination only.
- The other destination (central app) is completely unaffected (59/59 OK).
- The single "OK" on the edge path during the fault is a timing race
  (a probe request that was mid-flight at the moment of route injection).

CONCLUSION:
Reachability failure at one destination is independent of session and
registration state. This is the expected result and demonstrates that
a "network unreachable" condition is not the same as a "UE offline"
condition. Monitoring that only checks UE registration would miss this
fault; application-level probes detect it immediately.
